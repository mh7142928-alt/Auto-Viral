# Privacy Policy for Auto Publisher

**Last updated: September 28, 2026**

This Privacy Policy describes how the Auto Publisher application handles your information when you use our automated content creation and posting service.

## 1. Information We Collect
Auto Publisher is an automated tool designed to manage and upload multimedia content (videos, captions, and titles) created by the user to their authorized social media accounts (such as TikTok, YouTube, and Instagram).
- We only access the permissions explicitly granted by the user via official OAuth2 protocols (such as video upload and basic profile information).
- We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal data to any third parties.

## 2. How We Use Information
The collected tokens and credentials are used solely for:
- Authenticating the user's account to publish user-approved video content.
- Querying publishing status and media upload confirmation.

## 3. Data Storage and Security
All tokens and credentials are encrypted and stored securely within the user's private environment or private repository secrets. No data is transmitted to unauthorized external servers.

## 4. User Control & Revocation
You can revoke Auto Publisher's access to your TikTok account at any time through your TikTok account security and app permissions settings.
