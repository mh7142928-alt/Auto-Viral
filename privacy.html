Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

Auto Viral respects your privacy. This policy describes how our application collects and handles user data.

1. Data Collection

Auto Viral only accesses your social media account credentials (such as OAuth tokens) strictly for the purpose of uploading user-authorized videos directly to your designated profiles.

2. Data Usage

We do not sell, trade, or share your account credentials or personal information with third parties. Tokens are stored securely in encrypted environment variables.

3. Data Deletion

You can revoke access to your social media accounts at any time through your Google, Meta, or TikTok account security settings.
